Who Am I?

Hello, my name is Abd from Gaza. I am 20 years old. Like everyone else, I have dreams and I strive to achieve them, but I have not been able to. The war has taken so much from our lives—our years, our dreams, and people who were very dear to us. My home was destroyed, and I no longer feel there is hope in life.

My University Dream

I am now 20 years old and have not been able to achieve anything. I kindly ask for your support in helping me enroll in university. My father is 47 years old and suffers from a chronic heart condition that makes it very difficult for him to move and work. I carry so much pain, but I still hold on to a small hope.
